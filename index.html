how it works

four steps, all of them counting. no model, no inference.

  1. read every token created on Robinhood Chain in the last hour name, symbol and the wallet that deployed it
  2. split names into words and drop the meaningless ones "coin", "token", "the", chain names — the full stop list is in the repository
  3. compare each word against how often it normally appears built from the last 14 days of the chain's own history
  4. report a word only if it beats all three thresholds far above its normal rate, enough separate launches, enough separate wallets

thresholds

changing these changes the answer. they are chosen, not derived.

what this cannot do

it matches strings, not meaning. a narrative carried under different words is missed completely. a narrative where everyone copied one name is found in seconds.

a word appearing often is not the signal. a word appearing far more often than it normally does is the signal. "dog" can lead every hour and never count, because "dog" always leads.

the thresholds are a judgement call. someone who groups the same launches differently gets a different answer, and that is a property of counting, not a defect hidden from you.

this page shows what was created, not what is worth buying. a spike in token names is evidence that people are launching the same word, and nothing else.
